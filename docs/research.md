This build's distinct unit is the network post, its revision-bound approval and its exported metric history. It is not a social network and does not rebuild network access or listening data. Selection requires useful offline planning and review, not OAuth or a front end.

## Scope

Owned post plans, approvals, campaign review and performance from exports. No inbox, media processing, listening dataset, live publishing or automatic network metric collection. Unknown metrics are preserved. Enterprise DNA scopes any necessary connections and front end in a custom version. No claim of complete suite parity or immediate cancellation is justified.
