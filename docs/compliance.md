# Review checks and their sources

Checked 1 November 2026. These are evidence reminders, not legal certification or a substitute for a reviewer inspecting the finished advertisement. No legal notice, payment or regulated filing is produced.

## Advertising identification

AD-DISCLOSURE reports advertising records with no disclosure review evidence. The operator must mark advertising=true when applicable. An unmarked record is outside this check; the software cannot infer commercial arrangements from text.

- New Zealand: [ASA identification guidance](https://asa.co.nz/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/Quick-Guide-Identification-of-Ads-1.pdf) explains that advertising must be recognisable and discusses visibility, placement and non-cash arrangements. Record how the reviewer established that identification is clear. A stored label alone does not check visual prominence.
- Australia: [AANA Code of Ethics, section 2.7](https://aana.com.au/code-of-ethics/) requires distinguishable advertising. The practice note says an explicit label is not always needed when the commercial nature is obvious. Record that assessment or the disclosure used; the CLI does not demand a specific hashtag.

Profiles use NZ or AU for source selection. Set the correct jurisdiction before relying on a report. Cross-border work needs both jurisdictions reviewed by the operator.

## House rules

HOUSE-CLAIMS flags advertising without a claim evidence reference. HOUSE-RIGHTS flags a known asset-rights expiry before today or the intended use date. These are conservative operating policies, not statutory deadlines. Missing rights dates do not mean rights are cleared; inspect the licence and record its scope separately. The generic claims reference must be checked by a person for each statement.

Approval requires an owner, a planned date, current evidence checks and a human decision on the exact revision. Changes to reviewed content, campaign, timing, disclosure, claim evidence or rights invalidate approval. This is a workflow policy, not a legal signature. No visual review, platform policy review, sector-specific advertising review or factual substantiation is automated.
